## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
**1. Do I need an existing Amazon business to take this course?**
No, the course covers the foundational steps of setting up an Amazon seller account and establishing your business entity. It is suitable for beginners.
**2. How much money do I need to start Amazon Wholesale?**
Wholesale requires more upfront capital than Arbitrage, but less risk than Private Label. You generally need at least $2,000 to $5,000 to place your initial minimum order quantities (MOQs) with brands.
**3. Is this course only about using the SmartScout software?**
While it heavily features the software as the primary sourcing tool, a large portion of the course focuses on communication, negotiation, and business operations that apply regardless of the software you use.
**4. How is Wholesale different from Online Arbitrage?**
In Online Arbitrage, you buy discounted products from retail websites (like Walmart or Target) and resell them on Amazon. It is difficult to scale. In Wholesale, you buy directly from the brand or their authorized distributor at true wholesale prices, allowing for consistent, scalable inventory replenishment.
**5. What if brands say they don't want any more Amazon sellers?**
This is the most common objection, and the course dedicates significant time to overcoming it. You learn to pitch yourself not as "just another seller," but as a brand manager who will protect their MAP pricing and optimize their listings.
