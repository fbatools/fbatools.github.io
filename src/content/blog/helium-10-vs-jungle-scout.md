## 1. Product Research: Black Box vs. Opportunity Finder
Finding a winning product is the first hurdle every Amazon seller faces. Both software suites offer massive databases of Amazon's catalog, but they filter data differently.
**Helium 10 Black Box**  
Black Box is an absolute powerhouse. It allows you to filter over 2 billion Amazon products using hyper-specific criteria. You can search by search volume, monthly revenue, review count, shipping tier, and even the number of images a competitor has. If you want to find a product making $10,000 a month with fewer than 50 reviews, Black Box will find it in seconds.
**Jungle Scout Opportunity Finder**  
Jungle Scout takes a slightly different approach. While they also have a product database, their *Opportunity Finder* focuses on finding profitable *niches* rather than individual products. It helps beginners identify broad categories that have high demand and low competition. 
**The Winner:** Tie. Jungle Scout is great for broad niche discovery, but Helium 10's Black Box is better for pinpointing exact ASINs to compete against.

## 2. Keyword Research: Cerebro vs. Keyword Scout
If you want to rank on Page 1 of Amazon, your keyword research must be flawless. This is where the two tools begin to separate significantly.
**Helium 10 Cerebro**  
Cerebro is widely considered the best reverse-ASIN tool on the market. You simply paste a top competitor's ASIN into Cerebro, and it will spit out exactly which keywords are generating their sales. In 2026, Cerebro's "CPR" (Cerebro Product Rank) score tells you exactly how many units you need to give away or sell over an 8-day period to rank on page 1 for that specific keyword.
**Jungle Scout Keyword Scout**  
Keyword Scout is a highly capable tool that pulls exact search volumes and historical trends. However, its reverse-ASIN capabilities do not dig quite as deep as Cerebro, and it lacks the highly specific CPR metric that makes Helium 10 so actionable for product launches.
**The Winner:** Helium 10. Cerebro is simply unmatched in the FBA industry.

## 3. Listing Optimization and Copywriting
Once you have your keywords, you need to write a listing that converts browsers into buyers while keeping the Amazon A9 algorithm happy.
**Helium 10 (Frankenstein & Scribbles)**  
Helium 10 breaks this into a two-step process. First, you use *Frankenstein* to deduplicate and clean your massive keyword lists. Then, you import that list into *Scribbles* (or their newer Listing Builder). As you write your title and bullet points, Scribbles crosses off keywords in real-time, ensuring you never miss a high-volume search term.
**Jungle Scout (Listing Builder)**  
Jungle Scout also features a Listing Builder that integrates with their keyword tool. It offers an AI-assisted copywriting feature that is very intuitive for beginners who struggle with writing marketing copy.
**The Winner:** Helium 10 for pure SEO control, though Jungle Scout gets an honorable mention for ease of use.
