### What is Helium 10 Magnet?
Helium 10 Magnet is a powerful keyword research tool specifically designed for Amazon sellers. Unlike reverse ASIN tools, Magnet requires you to input a broad "seed" keyword (for example, "garlic press" or "yoga mat"). It then scans Amazon's actual search database and returns a massive list of related keywords. 
These results are enriched with crucial data points such as search volume, competing products, Magnet IQ Score, and Title Density. This wealth of information empowers you to make data-driven decisions on which keywords to target in your listings and PPC campaigns.

### Step-by-Step Guide: How to Use Magnet

#### 1. Enter Your Seed Keyword
Start by brainstorming a broad keyword that accurately describes your product. Enter this seed keyword into the Magnet search bar. You can even enter up to 200 keywords separated by commas if you want to perform a bulk search. Select your target marketplace (e.g., Amazon.com) and hit "Get Keywords."

#### 2. Analyze the Top-Level Data
At the top of the results page, you'll see a summary box. This provides an overview of the seed keyword, including its exact search volume, broad search volume, and the total number of keywords Magnet has found. Pay attention to the "Word Frequency" section, which shows you the individual words that appear most often across all the keyword phrases found. This is fantastic for identifying core terms to include in your backend search terms or title.

#### 3. Filter Your Results for Maximum Impact
Magnet often returns thousands of keywords. To find the most actionable ones, you need to use the filters:

- **Search Volume:** Set a minimum search volume (e.g., 300 or 500) to ensure the keywords you target actually have enough customer interest to drive sales.
- **Competing Products:** To find lower-competition keywords, set a maximum limit on the number of competing products.
- **Magnet IQ Score:** The Magnet IQ Score is a proprietary metric that compares a keyword's search volume to its number of competing products. A high score means there is a high search volume but low competition—these are your absolute goldmines. Sort your filtered list by Magnet IQ Score in descending order.

#### 4. Identify Long-Tail Keywords
Long-tail keywords are longer, more specific phrases (e.g., "stainless steel garlic press with cleaner brush"). While they may have lower individual search volumes, they often boast much higher conversion rates because the customer intent is highly specific. Use the "Word Count" filter to show keywords with 3 or more words to uncover these highly profitable, long-tail variations.

### Deep Dive: Advanced Magnet Strategies

#### The Title Density Filter
Title Density tells you how many of the top-ranking products for a specific keyword have that exact keyword in their title. If a keyword has high search volume but a low Title Density (e.g., less than 5), it means competitors are not optimizing for this exact phrase. If you put this keyword in your product title, you have a very high chance of ranking on page one quickly.

#### Exporting to Frankenstein
Once you have curated a list of highly relevant, high-opportunity keywords, you can export them directly to Helium 10's Frankenstein tool. Frankenstein is a keyword processor that will de-duplicate your list and format it perfectly, making it easy to paste into your Amazon backend search terms or Listing Builder.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
**1. What is the difference between Magnet and Cerebro?**
Cerebro is a reverse ASIN tool where you input a competitor's product ID to see what keywords they rank for. Magnet is a keyword expansion tool where you input a seed word to find a vast array of related search terms from Amazon's database. Both are essential for complete research.
**2. What is a good Magnet IQ Score?**
There is no universal "good" score as it varies heavily by category. However, you should use it relative to other keywords in your search. Sort by the highest score; those are mathematically your best opportunities for high traffic and low competition.
**3. Does Magnet work for international Amazon marketplaces?**
Yes, Magnet supports multiple Amazon marketplaces including the UK, Canada, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, India, and more. Always ensure you have the correct marketplace selected before running a search.
**4. How often should I use Magnet?**
It is recommended to run a Magnet search before launching a product, and then periodically (every 1-3 months) to catch new search trends or long-tail keywords that consumers have started using.
**5. Can I use Magnet for Amazon PPC campaigns?**
Absolutely. The long-tail, high-IQ score keywords you find in Magnet are perfect candidates for exact match PPC campaigns, often yielding a lower Cost Per Click (CPC) and higher Return on Ad Spend (ROAS).
