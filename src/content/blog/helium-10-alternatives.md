## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Can I use multiple tools at the same time?**

A: Yes, many advanced sellers use a combination, such as Helium 10 for Private Label optimization and SmartScout for wholesale brand research. 

**Q: Are these tools completely accurate?**

A: No Amazon tool is 100% accurate, as Amazon does not share exact sales data. These tools use proprietary algorithms to estimate sales based on Best Sellers Rank (BSR). However, they are highly accurate estimates and crucial for making informed decisions.

**Q: Do I really need software to sell on Amazon?**

A: Technically no, but practically yes. Doing product research manually in 2026 is like trying to chop down a tree with a butter knife. Your competitors are using data; you need to use data too.

**Q: Is there a free Helium 10 alternative?**

A: Most robust tools are paid. However, AMZScout and Jungle Scout sometimes offer basic free estimators, and Helium 10 itself has a free tier with limited functionality.

**Q: How do I cancel my Helium 10 subscription if I switch?**

A: You can easily downgrade or cancel your plan within the Helium 10 billing dashboard. Just be sure to export any critical keyword data before you do!
