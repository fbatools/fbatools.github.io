## Step 1: Product Research (Black Box & Xray)
Everything starts with finding a winning product. If you pick a bad product, no amount of marketing or optimization will save you. 

### Black Box
Start in Black Box. This is your idea generator. Instead of blindly searching Amazon, Black Box lets you filter the entire Amazon catalog based on data.

- **Set Your Criteria:** You want to find products with high revenue but low competition. A good starting point is filtering for products with a minimum monthly revenue of $5,000 and a maximum review count of 150.
- **Filter by Category:** Stick to beginner-friendly categories like Home & Kitchen, Pet Supplies, or Office Products. Avoid electronics or supplements.
- **Analyze Results:** Black Box will spit out hundreds of ideas. Scroll through and look for products that are small, simple, and not dominated by major brand names.

### Xray (Chrome Extension)
Once you find an interesting idea in Black Box, type that product's main keyword into the regular Amazon search bar. Then, open the Helium 10 Xray Chrome extension. Xray will instantly analyze all the products on that page, showing you their exact sales, revenue, reviews, and fees. This validates if the entire niche is profitable or just one lucky seller.

## Step 2: Keyword Research (Cerebro & Magnet)
Once you have validated a product idea and decided to source it, you need to know how customers are searching for it. This is where keyword research comes in.

### Cerebro (Reverse ASIN Lookup)
Cerebro is arguably the most powerful tool in Helium 10. You take the ASIN (Amazon Standard Identification Number) of your top competitor and plug it into Cerebro. 

- **The Magic Trick:** Cerebro will reverse-engineer their listing and show you exactly which keywords they are ranking for and where their sales are coming from. 
- **Identify Targets:** Look for highly relevant keywords with decent search volume where your competitors might be weak. These are the keywords you will target in your own listing.

### Magnet (Keyword Expansion)
While Cerebro shows you what competitors are doing, Magnet helps you find related keywords they might have missed. Enter a broad keyword (like "garlic press"), and Magnet will generate thousands of related, long-tail keyword variations. This helps you build a massive master list of keywords.

## Step 3: Listing Optimization (Frankenstein & Scribbles)
Now that you have a massive list of keywords, you need to weave them into your product listing (Title, Bullet Points, and Description) so Amazon's algorithm knows what your product is.

### Frankenstein (Keyword Processor)
Take all the keywords you found in Cerebro and Magnet and paste them into Frankenstein. This tool removes duplicates, common words (like "and", "the"), and organizes everything into a clean, distinct list of powerful root words.

### Scribbles (Listing Builder)
Take your clean list from Frankenstein and move it to Scribbles. As you write your product Title and Bullet Points, Scribbles will track which keywords you have used and cross them off the list. This ensures you never miss a crucial, high-volume keyword in your listing. It's like having an SEO expert looking over your shoulder.
